## Question

Props are external inputs; state is internal mutable data.

## Expected Answer

Props are external inputs; state is internal mutable data.

## Deep Explanation

Props (Properties) are read-only attributes passed from parent components to child components to configure them. They are immutable from the perspective of the child component.
