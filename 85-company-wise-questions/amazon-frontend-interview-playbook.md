# T8514 · Amazon Frontend Technical Interview Playbook: Leadership Principles, E-Commerce & Micro-Frontends

**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Companies Asked:** Amazon  
**Category:** Company-wise Questions  
**Concepts:** amazon, frontend-interview, playbook, leadership-principles, e-commerce  

## Comprehensive Guide

This playbook details the specific structure, grading rubrics, and technical expectations evaluated during **Amazon Front End Engineer (FEE II / Senior FEE)** technical interviews.

### Key Evaluation Areas
1. **Amazon Leadership Principles (STAR Method)**: Customer Obsession, Ownership, Invent and Simplify, Bias for Action in UI engineering context.
2. **E-Commerce Checkout Architecture**: Idempotency, payment security, multi-step checkout state machine, and cart syncing.
3. **Micro-Frontends**: Independent team deployments using Webpack Module Federation or iframe wrappers.
