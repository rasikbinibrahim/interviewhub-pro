## Comprehensive Guide

This playbook details the specific structure, grading rubrics, and technical expectations evaluated during **Uber Senior Frontend Engineer** technical interviews.

### Key Evaluation Areas
1. **Interactive Mapping & Geospatial Visualization**: Deck.gl / Mapbox GL integration, rendering 100,000+ real-time driver locations smoothly with WebGL shaders.
2. **Real-Time Driver Tracking**: WebSockets and Server-Sent Events (SSE) handling high-frequency GPS coordinate updates with smooth marker interpolation.
3. **Machine Coding**: Real-time autocomplete location input with debouncing and spatial map bounds centering.
